About QRPay.so

Simple, fast, and secure QR payments for Somalia’s mobile money economy.

Our Story

QRPay.so was created to solve a real problem in Somalia’s digital economy. Today, most payments are done via mobile money (EVC Plus & eDahab), but users still manually type numbers for every transaction.


We built QRPay.so to remove that friction — turning payment details into a simple QR code that anyone can scan instantly.

The Problem

Slow manual number entry
Typing errors in payments
Poor customer experience
Lost business opportunities

Who It’s For

Small Businesses
Street Vendors
Mosques & NGOs
Schools
Freelancers

Our Vision

To make payments in Somalia as simple as scanning a QR code — faster, safer, and accessible for everyone.
